Key data-engineering challenge: building leakage-free, point-in-time temporal training and evaluation data from a noisy financial panel, where look-ahead in features and labels, unadjusted corporate actions that violate price-limit bands, and split design silently inflate downstream ML results. Contributions: (1) a point-in-time feature store with a price-band corporate-action audit, an IC leakage scan validated by a planted canary and a truncation-invariance test (Sec. III-IV); (2) a purged, embargoed walk-forward protocol applied to learners and combiner (Sec. V); (3) an experimental analysis with released artifacts, plus incremental-update and scale-up efficiency results (Sec. VIII). Builds on VLDB/SIGMOD/ICDE work on data validation (Schelter, PVLDB 2018), feature stores (Orr, PVLDB 2021), ML data lifecycle (Polyzotis, SIGMOD Record 2018), data-preparation effects on ML (Guha, ICDE 2023) and time-series benchmarking (TFB, PVLDB 2024).
